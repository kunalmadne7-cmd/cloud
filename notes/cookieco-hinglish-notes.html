CookieCo Case: Problem Hai Kya?
Simple Bhasha Mein:

CookieCo = US ki ek badi company jo packaged cookies banati bhi hai aur bechti bhi hai.

Pichle 2–3 saal se iska profit gir raha hai, jabki baaki cookie industry achhe se grow kar rahi hai.

CookieCo BCG ke paas aayi hai: (1) problem ki jad (root cause) dhoondo, (2) usse ulta (reverse) kaise karein, batao.

Industry ↑healthy growth
vs
CookieCo ↓profit decline

Case Prompt (original):

"Our client is a national manufacturer and seller of packaged cookies in the U.S. In the last 2–3 years, they've seen a decline in profits whereas the rest of the industry has experienced healthy growth. They have come to us to identify the root cause of this issue and how they can reverse it."

Example:

Socho poore shehar mein mithai ki bikri badh rahi hai, par ek dukaan ka munafa gir raha hai. Matlab problem bazaar mein nahi, dukaan ke andar hai (shayad kharcha badh gaya).

4. Structure Ko 3/4 Se 4/4 Kaise Banayein?

① Costs PEHLE present karo

Generic profit tree mat padho. Bolo: "Mujhe lagta hai issue cost mein hai, wahin se shuru karta hoon." = hypothesis-driven.

② "High level" mat raho

Depth dikhao. Revenue → units, price, product mix, discounting, inflation ka impact. Costs → har bucket ke andar 2–3 items.

③ MECE rakho

Buckets overlap na karein. "Market" bucket mein revenue wali cheezein dobara mat daalo. (MECE = Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)

2. Fixed vs Variable Cost

  • Production kam ho ya zyada, utna hi lagta hai.
  • e.g. labour, warehouse ka kiraya, distribution setup.

Analogy: dukaan ka kiraya. Bikri ho ya na ho, dena padega.

VARIABLE COST

  • Jitni cookies, utna kharcha.
  • e.g. ingredients, raw materials.

Analogy: jitne parathe banaoge, utna atta lagega.

3. Macro Context (2020–2022): Bahar Kya Ho Raha Tha?
Pandemic + Recession→Labour kam→Supply ↓→Distribution MEHENGA

(labour = drivers, freight operators; demand utni hi rahi)

  • Raw materials bhi mehenge: labour ki availability kam + industry grow → ingredients ki demand zyada.
  • Supply–demand ka simple rule: cheez kam, chahne wale zyada → price premium.

Lekin ek twist! 🤔

Distribution mehenga hua to poori industry pe asar padega, sirf CookieCo pe nahi. Toh ye akela reason nahi ho sakta ki sirf hamara profit gira. Isliye data aate hi pivot.

Yaad rakho: Cost brainstorm karna ho to "journey" approach (bana → store → pack → bhejo) best hai. Isse kuch miss nahi hota aur structure apne aap MECE ban jaata hai.

Ed Ki Galti: Units!

Ed ne bola "12% se 22%", par exhibit $ million mein tha, % mein nahi. Rule: pehle units padho, phir % khud nikaal ke batao:

% Change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100

(20 − 12) ÷ 12 × 100 ≈ 67%

Storage & Warehousing: Andar Kya Hai?

Analogy:

Solutions: Inventory Loss Kaise Kam Karein?
Sawaal tha: "Can you suggest a couple of ways to bring them under control?" → yaani SOLUTIONS chahiye the, sirf problems nahi!
Problem → Fix → Analogy

1. Storage Conditions Kharab

Fix: Climate control: temperature aur humidity sahi rakho.

Caveat: achhi packaging ko bhi cookies bachani chahiye.

Analogy: fridge ka temperature galat ho to doodh jaldi phat jaata hai.

2. Inventory Management Weak

Problem: dabbe peeche kone mein pade reh gaye, yaad hi nahi rahe.

Fix: proper tracking system + FIFO (First In, First Out). LIFO ✗ (Ed ne pehle galti se LIFO bola, phir sudhaara.)

Analogy: ghar mein purana doodh pehle use karte ho, naya baad mein.

3. Over-Production / Kam Turnover

Fix: demand forecasting + analytics: kitna bikega, utna hi banao (recession mein log kam khareedte hain).

Analogy: shaadi mein guest list dekh ke khana banate ho, warna bacha khana waste.

4. Shelf Life Kam

Fix: recipe / packaging optimize karo taaki cookies zyada din fresh rahein.

Note: Ed ne ise last mein rakha. Pehle 2 pe zyada focus.

3. Better Version (Sample Script)

① Context: "CookieCo ka profit gir raha hai jabki market aur competitors grow kar rahe hain."

② Finding: "Problem cost mein hai. Storage & warehousing 2 saal mein ~$12M se ~$20M (~+67%) ho gaya, aur iski wajah hai inventory losses: cookies expire ho rahi hain."

③ Recommendations (teen): (1) FIFO inventory-management system, (2) warehouses mein sahi climate control, (3) demand forecasting se production ko demand ke saath match karna.

④ Risks (do): (1) savings aane se pehle upfront kharcha, (2) forecasting galat hua to stock-out.

⑤ Next steps (do): (1) warehouses ka audit, (2) SKU / location ke hisaab se loss ka size nikaalna.
