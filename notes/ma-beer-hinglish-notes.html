M&A Case: Problem Hai Kya?
Simple Bhasha Mein:

Ek badi international daaru company (Europe se, abhi sirf whisky banati hai) India ki ek chhoti local beer company ko khareedna (acquire) chahti hai.

Humein due diligence karni hai: kya ye deal sahi hai? Kya target company client ke liye "good fit" hai?

AcquirerEurope, whisky
khareedna→
TargetGoa, beer

Case Prompt (original):

"Your client is an international manufacturer of alcoholic beverages, and they're considering acquiring a local beer manufacturer in India. Evaluate the opportunity."

Example:

Jaise ek badi chocolate brand ek local namkeen company khareede: naya product bhi milega, aur local market ki samajh bhi. Par pehle check karo: paisa vasool hai ya nahi?

1. M&A Ki Basic Bhasha

Acquisition

Ek company doosri ko khareed leti hai (control le leti hai).

Due Diligence

Khareedne se pehle poori jaanch: financials, operations, risks.

Synergy

Saath aane se 1 + 1 = 3: jo fayda akele nahi milta.

Is case mein: Acquirer = client (khareedne wala). Target / Asset = local beer company (jise khareedna hai).
2. Interview Ka Flow (Ek Nazar Mein)
Recap→Target ke Qs→Acquirer ke Qs→30 sec: Structure→Financial→Operational→Synergies→Risks→Feedback
Style dekho: candidate ne har bucket se pehle 10 sec maange ("Can I take 10 seconds to build my approach?"), phir us bucket ko detail mein khola = bucket-by-bucket deep dive.

3. Yaad Rakhne Ka Checklist

TARGET ke liye (slide pe "CPCC" type)

  • Company: financials, geography
  • Product: kya bechte hain, price segment
  • Customer: kaun khareedta hai
  • Competitor: market mein aur kaun

ACQUIRER ke liye

  • Kahan hai, kya bechta hai
  • Objective: deal kyun? (growth, portfolio, market entry)
  • Kya laata hai: brand, capital, distribution
⚠ Miss hue sawaal (feedback): beer glass bottle ya can mein? Bikri B2B ya B2C? Candidate ne B2C assume kiya, theek tha, par clarify karna chahiye tha (page 6).

Financial + Operational Feasibility
1. Financial Feasibility: "Paisa Banega?"

Revenue

  • Pichle kuch saal ka revenue badh raha hai?
  • Market share bhi badh raha?
  • → West mein product successful / viable hai ya nahi

Cost Structure

  • Raw materials / procurement
  • Production
  • Sales cost: alcohol industry mein bahut high hota hai

Metrics

  • IRR (aur ROI / NPV)
  • Kya ye client ke objective se match karta hai?
⚠ Feedback: financial bucket mein aur add kar sakte the: cap table, interest payments, profitability, aur project ka expected NPV.
2. Operational Feasibility: "Business Chalega?"

① Target Audience (kaun khareedta?)

Mass market, sasta → sab tarah ke drinkers. India mein log alcohol pe kam kharch karna pasand karte hain → attractive. Par occasional drinkers shayad premium beer lein.

② Value Proposition / USP (kyun khareedta?)

Taste? Quality? Ya packaging? Goa = tourists, aur tourist aksar packaging dekh ke khareedte hain, taste se kam.

③ Channel (kahan bikta?)

Retail shops / thekas vs bars, pubs, restaurants. Mass product → zyada thekas mein, premium bars mein kam.

④ Production

Production capacity kitni hai? Koi unutilized capacity hai jise use karke revenue badha sakein? Process robust hai?

⑤ Talent / Workforce

  • Leadership: alcohol industry ka kitna experience?
  • Sales team: B2B selling mostly sales team se hoti hai → kitni efficient?
  • General workforce: khush hain? Naraz workforce = management mein problem ka signal.

Example:

Ghar khareedne se pehle dekhte ho: EMI chalegi? (financial) aur rehne layak hai? (operational). Dono check zaroori.

Synergies + Risks: 1 + 1 = 3?
1. Teen Synergies (Candidate Ke)

① Product Portfolio

  • Whisky (client) + Beer (target) = 2 products
  • Channels / partners se better deals negotiate
  • Brand awareness zyada

② Knowledge

  • Goa ki local demand, seasonal ups-downs
  • Compliance: alcohol laws har state mein alag
  • Local beer ko West se baaki India mein le jaana

③ Risk Diversification

  • Kuch mausam / events mein beer zyada, kuch mein whisky
  • Goa mein concerts, events
  • Dono hon to risk kam

Analogy:

Chai ki dukaan wala samose bhi rakh le: same customer, same dukaan, zyada bikri. Garmi mein lassi, sardi mein chai → saal bhar income (diversification).

Par dhyan: samose ke liye alag supplier, alag udhaar ki shartein, alag storage chahiye. Wahi baat yahan beer vs whisky mein (credit cycle, warehousing alag).

⚠ Par har synergy sach nahi hoti! (Feedback)

  • Distributors ke credit cycles alag: local beer co. vs MNC whisky co. → ye synergy expect mat karo.
  • Warehousing: beer aur whisky ki storage needs bilkul alag.
  • Restaurants ke menu fixed hote hain, overnight nahi badalte → cross-selling retail / thekas mein chalega, bars / restaurants mein nahi.
2. Risks (Slide Se)

Licensing

Alcohol = heavily regulated. Har state ke license aur laws alag. Goa ka license baaki states mein kaam nahi aayega.

Dilution

Slide pe sirf naam hai, detail nahi. Socho, jaise brand dilution (premium image pe asar) ya ownership / value dilution (deal ke baad hisse ki value).

Yaad rakho: synergy bolte waqt realistic raho. Har synergy ke saath socho: "Kya sach mein ho sakta hai? Kahan nahi chalega?" Interviewer isi pe pakadta hai.

Pehle East, South ya North India kyun nahi? West / Goa hi kyun?

2. Key Takeaways (Interview Se Pehle Padho!)
  1. Clarifying Qs mein Target + Acquirer dono cover karo, shuru se hi.
  2. Product ka format (glass / can) aur channel mix (B2B / B2C) poochho.
  3. Structure: Financial → Operational → Synergies → Risks.
  4. Financial mein ROI, IRR, NPV, cap table, interest mat bhoolo.
  1. Operational mein saare stakeholders (distributors bhi!).
  2. Synergies realistic rakho: credit cycle, warehousing, menu jaise limits socho.
  3. Deal ko pre → during → post lifecycle mein dekho.
  4. Har bucket se pehle chhota pause, end mein check-in karo.
