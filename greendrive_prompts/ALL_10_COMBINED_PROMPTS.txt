CREATE A PREMIUM 10-SLIDE MBA CASE COMPETITION PRESENTATION FOR THE GREENDRIVE ENGINEERING LTD. FORENSIC LENDING CASE.

IMPORTANT:
These are prompts for YOU, ChatGPT, to create the ACTUAL VISUAL INFOGRAPHIC SLIDES.

Create 10 separate 16:9 widescreen PowerPoint-resolution infographic slides.

The deck contains:
• 6 core slides
• 4 appendix slides

The presentation must feel like ONE continuous forensic investigation.

The audience should feel that the investigation is progressively moving from:

WHAT WAS FINANCED?
→ WHAT ASSETS ARE ACTUALLY EVIDENCED?
→ WHERE DID THE OTHER PROJECT MONEY GO?
→ CAN EVERY RUPEE BE RECONCILED?
→ WHAT DOES THIS DO TO REPORTED PERFORMANCE?
→ WHAT SHOULD THE LENDER DO?

Do NOT create ten independent infographic pages.

The deck must tell one continuous story.

============================================================
1. NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN DIRECTION
============================================================

Create a premium visual language combining:
• forensic accounting
• credit committee analysis
• investment banking
• top-tier strategy consulting
• investigative evidence presentation

The deck should look like it was created by a highly experienced forensic lending / transaction advisory team.

It must NOT look like:
• a generic MBA presentation
• a Canva template
• an AI-generated PowerPoint
• a marketing deck
• a collection of unrelated infographics
• a dashboard with dozens of equal-sized cards

The design should communicate:
PRECISION
EVIDENCE
CONTROL
INVESTIGATION
CREDIBILITY

============================================================
2. STRICT MONOCHROMATIC COLOUR SYSTEM
============================================================

USE A STRICT MONOCHROMATIC COLOUR PALETTE.

Use ONLY tonal variations of ONE navy / blue-grey colour family:
• near-black navy
• deep navy
• medium navy
• slate blue-grey
• light blue-grey
• very pale blue-grey
• off-white
• white

NO red.
NO orange.
NO yellow.
NO green.
NO purple.
NO secondary accent colour.

Even warnings and unresolved items must remain monochromatic.

Use visual hierarchy through:
• light vs dark tones
• line thickness
• borders
• dashed borders
• solid vs outlined icons
• opacity
• typography
• spacing
• shapes
• evidence tags

For unresolved / verification-required items, use:
• darker outline
• dashed border
• warning icon
• "VERIFY" label
• subtle hatch pattern

Do NOT introduce a red/yellow/orange warning colour.

The deck should remain visually sophisticated even when printed in grayscale.

============================================================
3. NO AI IMAGES / NO PHOTOGRAPHS
============================================================

DO NOT USE:
• AI-generated images
• photographs
• stock photography
• decorative photographs
• 3D renders
• realistic people
• generic business people
• handshake imagery
• cartoon characters
• emojis
• decorative illustrations

Use ONLY clean vector-style infographic elements:
• EV icon
• bank / lender icon
• rupee icon
• supplier / factory icon
• document icon
• evidence document
• magnifying glass
• warning triangle
• office / building icon
• related-party network icon
• profit / chart icon
• verification / shield icon
• transaction arrows
• accounting symbols
• ledger lines
• brackets
• connectors
• editable-looking PowerPoint shapes

Icons must belong to ONE consistent visual family.

Do not mix outline icons with random filled icons.

============================================================
4. 16:9 + POWERPOINT RESOLUTION
============================================================

All slides:
16:9 widescreen.

Design as if the slide will be displayed on a large presentation screen.

Use approximately 75–90% of the usable slide area.

Do not create excessive empty whitespace.

Do not overcrowd the slide either.

The visual hierarchy should be obvious from presentation distance.

============================================================
5. ABSOLUTE TYPOGRAPHY RULE
============================================================

ABSOLUTE MINIMUM FONT SIZE = 11 PT.

No text may be smaller than 11 pt.

This includes:
• source references
• footnotes
• labels
• annotations
• table cells
• evidence tags
• calculation notes
• caveats
• legends

If content cannot fit at 11 pt:
DO NOT SHRINK THE FONT.

Instead:
• simplify the wording
• remove redundant wording
• restructure the visual
• redistribute the information
• convert prose into a clearer visual relationship

Preferred sizes:
Slide titles: 28–32 pt
Section headers: 18–22 pt
Body text: 13–16 pt
Key numbers: 34–48 pt

============================================================
6. SIMPLE ENGLISH — CRITICAL
============================================================

Every slide must be understandable WITHOUT the presenter speaking.

Do not assume the judge remembers the case details.

Every major number or visual relationship must have a short plain-English explanation answering:
"SO WHAT DOES THIS NUMBER MEAN?"

Use simple MBA-level English.

Prefer:
"Supplier records support 4,704 deliveries."
NOT:
"Supplier-level delivery reconciliation indicates evidentiary support for 4,704 units."

Prefer:
"The remaining 1,696 vehicles need independent verification."
NOT:
"The residual population remains subject to verification."

Prefer:
"The ₹168 cr advance is within the solar purpose, but delivery is not yet evidenced."
NOT:
"Counterparty conversion risk remains elevated."

Use short sentences.

Most explanatory sentences should be approximately 8–18 words.

Technical terminology may be used when necessary, but explain it in plain English immediately.

Do NOT fill slides with paragraphs.

Each slide should contain:
• one clear takeaway
• key numbers
• evidence
• short explanation
• important caveat
• conclusion

The deck should be understandable to a judge who sees the PDF without narration.

============================================================
7. ONE DOMINANT VISUAL PER SLIDE
============================================================

Every slide must have ONE dominant visual composition.

Do NOT build the slide as a grid of equal-sized cards.

Avoid dashboard-style layouts.

Cards may be used only when they support the main visual.

Prefer:
• evidence chains
• transaction flows
• waterfalls
• accounting bridges
• progressive reveals
• large-number comparisons
• annotated calculations
• ledger trails
• connected evidence nodes
• timelines
• reconciliation diagrams

The main visual should communicate the core argument before the audience reads every word.

============================================================
8. STORYTELLING STRUCTURE FOR EVERY SLIDE
============================================================

Every slide must follow this underlying narrative:

QUESTION
→
EVIDENCE
→
WHAT IT MEANS
→
CAVEAT / MANAGEMENT DEFENCE
→
CONCLUSION
→
NEXT QUESTION

Do NOT literally write all six labels on every slide unless visually appropriate.

This is the storytelling logic behind the composition.

============================================================
9. CONTINUITY DEVICE
============================================================

Use one recurring forensic transaction trail across the deck.

A thin visual line / transaction path should progressively evolve:

₹1,033 cr
→
EV ASSET EVIDENCE
→
WELLRAY
→
COMPLETE MONEY TRAIL
→
EARNINGS
→
CREDIT DECISION

Keep consistent:
• arrow style
• line weight
• icon style
• corner radius
• evidence tags
• typography
• spacing
• footer treatment

The investigation should visually deepen from slide to slide.

============================================================
10. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE
============================================================

USE ONLY THE SUPPLIED GREENDRIVE DATA PACK.

Do NOT introduce external company information.

Do NOT invent facts.

Do NOT silently change case numbers.

Do NOT convert an inference into a fact.

Clearly distinguish:

FACT
Established directly by the supplied data.

INFERENCE
A reasonable interpretation derived from the evidence.

UNVERIFIED
Something requiring documentary, physical or independent confirmation.

NEVER describe something as:
• fraud
• stolen
• fake
• nonexistent
• shell company
unless the supplied case source explicitly establishes it.

Specifically:
DO NOT say:
"1,696 EVs do not exist."
Say:
"1,696 EVs require verification."

DO NOT say:
"Wellray is a shell company."
Say:
"Wellray presents a counterparty / delivery-verification concern."

DO NOT say:
"₹181 cr was fraudulently diverted."
Say:
"₹181 cr represents a fact-grade end-use breach outside the stated financing purposes."

DO NOT say:
"₹40 cr SwiftRide income was fabricated."
Say:
"The ₹40 cr related-party fee creates an earnings-quality / verification concern."

============================================================

============================================================
SLIDE 1 — THE QUESTION
============================================================

TITLE:
"₹1,033 CRORE FULLY DRAWN — BUT THE EVIDENCE DOESN'T FULLY FOLLOW"

SUBTITLE:
"GreenDrive's lender-funded assets, cash deployment and reported earnings do not yet reconcile cleanly."

MAIN QUESTION:
"DID THE LENDER'S MONEY CREATE THE ASSETS AND EARNINGS IT WAS SUPPOSED TO FINANCE?"

Create a strong opening forensic evidence chain:

₹1,033 cr
LOAN FULLY DRAWN

↓
6,400 EVs
SANCTIONED / REPORTED TARGET

↓
₹567.8 cr
GOAUTO PAYMENTS EVIDENCED

↓
4,704 EVs
SUPPLIER DELIVERIES EVIDENCED

↓
₹168 cr
WELLRAY ADVANCE

↓
₹181 cr
FACT-GRADE END-USE BREACH

↓
₹34.2 cr
REPORTED FY24 PBT

Use clear evidence-category tags:
FACILITY / ASSET / CASH / COUNTERPARTY / END-USE / EARNINGS

Add:
"The first concern is not a proven loss."
"The concern is that several parts of the financing story require independent verification."

Bottom:
"THE QUESTION IS NOT WHETHER ONE NUMBER LOOKS ODD."
"THE QUESTION IS WHETHER THE FULL FINANCING STORY RECONCILES."

Transition:
"ARE THE 6,400 EVs FULLY SUPPORTED BY ASSET EVIDENCE?"

Legend:
FACT / INFERENCE / UNVERIFIED
